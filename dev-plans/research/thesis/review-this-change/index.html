What this page does not show

  
    
    
  • A careful reader finds every defect here in either framework. The diffs are short on purpose. The claim to test is about what happens at volume, when you are skimming the twentieth pull request of the day.
    • 
    
  • I wrote the app and planted the defects. No agent produced these changes, and no study has measured reviewers on them. The read-side eval is designed to measure that and has not run.
    • 
    
  • The React app is plain hooks. A team with lint rules, TypeScript, Redux Toolkit, or an AI reviewer has more than a bare diff. Redux names state changes, but not what triggers them or the effects around them.
    • 
    
  • The behaviour report is a prototype. Only --graph --base ships today. It sees the new request, hints at the lost guard through a removed sink, and says "no changes" on the drafts leak. Sygnal also costs more to write: agents use more context and touch more files.
